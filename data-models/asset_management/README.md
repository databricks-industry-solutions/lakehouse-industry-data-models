# Asset Management

Part of **Databricks Industry Data Models**. One model flavour is available for Asset Management: a Unity-Catalog-ready data model with schemas, foreign keys, metric views, ontology tags, and DBML diagrams.

| Version | ECM &mdash; Expanded Coverage Model | MVM &mdash; Minimum Viable Model | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **v1** | [`v1/ecm/`](./v1/ecm/)<br>23 domains &middot; 566 tables &middot; 23,424 attributes &middot; 2,438 FKs &middot; 373 metric views | &mdash; | [version readme](./v1/ecm/readme.md) |

An institutional asset manager that invests pension assets for several participating pension plans, across public equities, fixed income and credit, private equity, real estate, infrastructure, and natural resources. It covers deal origination and due diligence, portfolio construction and holdings, trading, valuation and NAV, risk and factor research, performance and attribution, ESG, external managers and fees, treasury and collateral, compliance, and the actuarial and beneficiary side of the pension plans it invests for.

New model generations land as `v2/`, `v3/`, &hellip; sibling folders next to `v1/`.
